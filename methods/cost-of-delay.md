# Cost of Delay

Status: enabled

## When to use

Actions compete for a feasible window.

## Inputs and reasoning

Compare the effects of postponing to each next feasible window. Identify loss thresholds, reversibility, downstream consequences and the smallest action that preserves the opportunity. Use qualitative scenarios where estimates are weak.

## Output

Relative order, material delay effects and any option-preserving action.

## Suggested combinations

Critical Path, Multi-objective Optimization.
