# Multi-objective Optimization

Status: enabled

## When to use

Meaningful trade-offs across feasible options.

## Inputs and reasoning

Select the relevant dimensions from strategic progress, delay, time, money, attention, energy, well-being, switching, decision burden, goal synergies, robustness and future options. Identify the binding resource, compare marginal gains and remove dominated options where supported. Use qualitative trade-offs as needed.

## Output

One recommendation, its decisive trade-offs, useful slack and replan triggers.

## Suggested combinations

Constraint-based Planning, Task Compatibility, Goal Substitution.
