# Constraint-based Planning

Status: enabled

## When to use

Fixed commitments, scarce resources or conditional preferences.

## Inputs and reasoning

Classify requirements as hard constraints, protective principles, contextual preferences, defaults or delegated choices. Check deadline, location, local time, preparation, transit, energy and external windows. Test moving, splitting or replacing actions while preserving the purpose of protective principles.

## Output

A feasible plan with buffers, assumptions and conflict resolution. Ask the user when hard constraints conflict or an important exception is needed.

## Suggested combinations

Cost of Delay, Goal Substitution.
